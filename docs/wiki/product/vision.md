# Vision

## In one sentence

NetGraph finds, among your contacts and your contacts' contacts, the people who can help with what you need right now, and who can introduce you.

## The problem

Professionals have hundreds of contacts spread across their phone, LinkedIn and Instagram. When they need something (a consulting mission, a client, an investor, an introduction at a company), the right person is often two contacts away. They don't know who it is, or who could make the introduction, and relationships go cold because nobody keeps track of them.

## How NetGraph works

1. **Import.** The user imports their existing contacts: phone address book, LinkedIn export, Instagram, Excel or CSV. Duplicates are merged.
2. **Ask.** The user writes a request, such as "Trouver ma prochaine mission de conseil en finance".
3. **Find the path.** NetGraph ranks the people who can help, including second-degree contacts, and shows the path: *Vous → Sara → Jérôme*, "Sara peut vous présenter — vous échangez chaque semaine".
4. **Reach out.** An agent suggests who to contact and when, based on how often the user talks to each person and on open requests. It drafts the message; **the user sends it**.

## Who it's for (as of 2026-10-25)

The first users are professionals in finance, consulting, investment and banking in Morocco (Casablanca), where business runs heavily on personal networks. The product and its copy are in French.

This is the founder's current target, not yet validated with users.

## What makes it different

- Starts from a **need**, not from a feed or a CRM pipeline.
- Uses the **strength of real relationships** (how often you talk) to pick the best path, not just job titles.
- **Warm introductions** through a mutual contact instead of cold outreach.

## What NetGraph will never do

- Send messages automatically on the user's behalf.
- Scrape LinkedIn, Instagram or any other platform.
- Show someone's contacts to anyone without their consent.

See [Data and privacy](../compliance/data-privacy.md).
