## The essentials

- **Colours:** `canvas` (#FAFAFA) page, `white` panels, `mist` (#F0EFF8) for one tinted band. **One brand colour:** `violet` (#4B3FCC) for every action, link and focus ring; it hovers to `violet-dark` (#342C96). `ink` (#0E0E0E) for headings, `slate` (#5A5A6E) for reading text. `signal` (#00C37A) means live or positive, and is never used alone as text.
- **Type:** Inter only. Semibold (600) for headings, 400 for reading, 500 for buttons and labels.
- **Shape:** radius 12px for buttons and inputs, 16px for cards and rows, 24px for large panels. Borders rather than shadows.
- **Paths in graphs:** "Vous" is an `ink` disc, the found path is a `violet` line, the person found fills `violet`, second-degree contacts are dashed gray until reached.
- **Icons:** 1.5px stroke icons in `violet` on `violet-light` tiles. People appear as initials, never photos. No emoji as icons.

## Voice

- French, formal *vous*, sentence case. CTAs end in "→" ("Rejoindre la beta →").
- Lead with the need, then the person, then the introduction.
- The agent proposes; the person sends: "Rien n'est envoyé sans votre accord".
- Concrete numbers over superlatives. Label demo data as fictional.
